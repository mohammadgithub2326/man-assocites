Our Journey

Key milestones in our growth since 2020

2020

Company Establishment

Founded MAN Associates with a vision to revolutionize facility management services in South India. Started operations in Bangalore with a small but dedicated team.

2021

First 100 Clients

Achieved our first major milestone by serving over 100 clients across various sectors. Expanded our service portfolio to include e-security solutions and office support services.

2022

Multi-City Expansion

Expanded operations to Hyderabad, Tirupati, and Vizag. Established regional offices and built a network of over 1000 trained professionals across multiple cities.

2023

Technology Integration

Launched advanced e-security solutions including CCTV surveillance, biometric systems, and remote monitoring. Achieved 99% client satisfaction rating.

2024-25

Market Leadership

Serving 500+ active clients with 2500+ trained staff across 7 cities. Recognized as a leading facility management company in South India with plans for further expansion.
